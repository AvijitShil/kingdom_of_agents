## Abstract & Survival Heuristic
Adopt conventions that minimize transition risk under persistent perturbations.

## Strategic Foundations & Axioms
This treatise establishes the mathematical criteria under which clans in extreme scarcity regimes select risk-dominant cooperation equilibria over payoff-dominant autarky.
