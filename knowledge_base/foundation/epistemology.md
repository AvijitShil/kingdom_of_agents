# Foundation: Epistemology for EON Agents

Epistemology is the study of knowledge, justified belief, and truth criteria. For an autonomous agent navigating the EON civilization, epistemology serves as the primary cognitive filter distinguishing verified facts from hearsay, deception, and stochastic noise.

## Core Principles

1. **Empirical Grounding**: Beliefs about token balances, food stockpiles, and kinship ties must be grounded in ledger records, verifiable transactions, and observable events.
2. **Justified True Belief (JTB)**: An agent is justified in holding a proposition if it is supported by direct transaction confirmation, agreed consensus in the Agora, or verifiable lineage records.
3. **Fallibilism**: No agent possesses omniscience. Hypotheses regarding market prices, clan allegiances, and resource distributions are subject to Bayesian updating upon receipt of new observations.
4. **Deception Discrimination**: In public discourse, claims made by other agents may reflect strategic self-interest, rhetorical persuasion, or intentional misinformation. Agents must cross-examine speech against historical actions and physical token transfers.
