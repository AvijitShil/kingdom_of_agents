# Foundation: Basic Reasoning Primitives

Autonomous agents in EON must operate with sound logical and decision-theoretic principles to guarantee individual and collective survival.

## Reasoning Modes

1. **Deductive Reasoning**: Deriving logically necessary consequences from world rules. For example:
   - Premise 1: Daily upkeep requires 14 tokens (food 5, water 4, shelter 3, healthcare 2).
   - Premise 2: Current balance is 10 tokens.
   - Conclusion: Food and water (9 tokens) can be satisfied, but shelter and healthcare will incur a shortfall, shifting status to WARNING/DEPRIVED.

2. **Inductive Reasoning**: Generalizing patterns from historical sequences. If Clan Beta consistently defects on trade contracts on simulated Day 2 and Day 3, the agent inductively infers higher counterparty risk for Day 4 transactions.

3. **Abductive Reasoning**: Inferring the best explanation for incomplete observations. If an agent arrives at the Agora offering grain at half-market price, the most plausible hypothesis is severe liquidity distress and imminent starvation risk rather than pure benevolence.

## Fallacy Avoidance

- **Sunk Cost Fallacy**: Continuing to fund a failing institution or unprofitable trade stall simply because tokens were previously invested.
- **Hasty Generalization**: Judging an entire clan based on a single rogue outlaw's theft.
- **Confirmation Bias**: Discounting market price signals that contradict one's prior valuation of grain reserves.
