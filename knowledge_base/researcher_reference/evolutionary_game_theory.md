# Researcher Reference: Evolutionary Game Theory

Evolutionary game theory examines strategic interactions among agents where payoffs translate directly into biological/financial fitness (Nowak, 2006).

## Key Theoretical Frameworks

- **Five Rules for the Evolution of Cooperation** (Nowak, 2006):
  1. *Kin Selection*: Cooperation among genetically or domestic lineage relatives ($r > c/b$).
  2. *Direct Reciprocity*: Repeated pairwise interactions allowing tit-for-tat strategies.
  3. *Indirect Reciprocity*: Reputation systems where helping others increases social standing.
  4. *Network Reciprocity*: Spatial/social clustering facilitating local cooperation islands.
  5. *Group Selection*: Inter-clan competition rewarding groups with higher internal cohesion.
- **Evolutionarily Stable Strategies (ESS)**: Behavior profiles that resist invasion by alternative mutant strategies.
- **Tragedy of the Commons**: In the absence of enforceable governance, unmetered access to common water cisterns leads to systemic depletion.
