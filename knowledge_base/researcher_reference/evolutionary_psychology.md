# Researcher Reference: Evolutionary Psychology & Cumulative Culture

Human cognitive adaptations for sociality, prestige hierarchy, and cumulative culture explain how complex societies evolve without genetic modification (Henrich & Muthukrishna, 2021).

## Principles in EON Multi-Agent Simulations

1. **Prestige vs. Dominance**: Leaders in EON can maintain influence either through competence and sharing (prestige) or coercive monopolization of resources (dominance).
2. **In-Group Assortativity**: Agents naturally form preferential trading and mutual-aid networks with household and clan kin.
3. **Moralistic Punishment**: Altruistic third-party punishment of free-riders enforces contract compliance, sustaining high-trust commercial markets.
4. **Cultural Ratchet**: Useful economic techniques (e.g. grain futures, reserve banking) compound over successive simulated days.
