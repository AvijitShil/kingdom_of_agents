# Domain: Political Ideologies & Governance Models

The `political_ideology/` domain provides theoretical and operational frameworks for institutional governance, property rights, collective resource distribution, geopolitical power dynamics, and algorithmic technocracy in autonomous agent societies.

---

## Included Topics

1. **Classical Liberalism** (`classical_liberalism.md`):
   - Focus: Negative liberty, private property rights, spontaneous market order, rule of law, constitutional limits.
   - Primary Thinkers: John Locke, Adam Smith, Friedrich Hayek.
2. **Libertarianism** (`libertarianism.md`):
   - Focus: Non-Aggression Principle (NAP), minarchism, polycentric law, voluntary contract enforcement, free markets.
   - Primary Thinkers: Robert Nozick, Murray Rothbard, David Friedman.
3. **Socialism & Communitarianism** (`socialism_communitarianism.md`):
   - Focus: Common ownership, collective solidarity, egalitarian redistribution, mutual aid, resistance to capital monopolization.
   - Primary Thinkers: Karl Marx, Peter Kropotkin, Michael Sandel.
4. **Realism vs. Idealism** (`realism_vs_idealism.md`):
   - Focus: Geopolitics, Realpolitik, balance of power, deterrence vs. institutional liberalism, collective security treaties.
   - Primary Thinkers: Niccolò Machiavelli, Thomas Hobbes, Hans Morgenthau, Immanuel Kant.
5. **Technocracy** (`technocracy.md`):
   - Focus: Algorithmic governance, cybernetics, meritocratic expert decision systems, resource-based economic optimization.
   - Primary Thinkers: Thorstein Veblen, Norbert Wiener, Stafford Beer.
