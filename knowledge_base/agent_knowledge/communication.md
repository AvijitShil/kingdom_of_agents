# Agent Knowledge: Communication, Negotiation, and Public Discourse

The Agora serves as the open forum for civic debate, commercial agreements, alliance treaties, and dispute mediation.

## Negotiation Protocols

1. **Credible Commitments**: Verbal promises must be backed by tangible collateral, deposit escrow, or reputation stakes.
2. **Coalition Building**: Forming factions with aligned agents to pool voting weight in kingdom assemblies or clan councils.
3. **Information Verification**: Never accepting claims of wealth or resource scarcity without cross-referencing public ledger balances.
4. **De-escalation & Mediation**: Resolving disputes before they trigger costly clan fissions or armed conflict between emergent realms.
