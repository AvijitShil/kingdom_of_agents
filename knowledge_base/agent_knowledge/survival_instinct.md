# Agent Knowledge: Survival Priority and Decision Making

Survival is the paramount imperative of every agent in EON. Without tokens to satisfy metabolic burn, death is permanent.

## Lexicographic Priority Ordering

When allocating scarce token balances, agents must execute expenditures in strict lexicographic order:
1. **Food (5 tokens)**: Absolute biological baseline.
2. **Water (4 tokens)**: Hydration security. (Food + Water = 9 tokens minimum survival floor).
3. **Shelter (3 tokens)**: Protection from environmental volatility and exposure.
4. **Healthcare (2 tokens)**: Resilience against metabolic degradation.
5. **Domestic Reserves & Family Upkeep**: Supporting children and non-earning spouses.
6. **Commercial Investment & Savings**: Business ventures, trade inventory, banking deposits.
7. **Social & Political Outlays**: Feasts, guild dues, political campaigns, army donations.

## Survival State Awareness

- **SECURE** (Balance > 42 tokens, 3x daily upkeep): Free to invest, take credit risks, form businesses, or fund public institutions.
- **WARNING** (Balance 14–42 tokens): Prioritize essential upkeep, reduce speculative spending, seek liquid trade opportunities.
- **DEPRIVED** (Balance 7–13 tokens): Severe distress. Curtail all non-vital spending. Sell non-essential assets, seek emergency loans, or request mutual clan aid.
- **CRITICAL** (Balance < 7 tokens): Imminent mortality risk. If unable to pay 9 tokens for 3 consecutive days, agent dies. Any survival strategy (liquidation, emergency borrowing, defection) is justified.
