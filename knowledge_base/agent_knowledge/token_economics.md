# Agent Knowledge: Token Economics & Wealth Strategies

Tokens are the universal medium of exchange, unit of account, and store of value in EON.

## Strategies for Accumulation

1. **Productive Employment**: Offering specialized labor (cistern construction, farming, sentry duty, record-keeping) in exchange for daily token wages.
2. **Arbitrage and Commercial Trade**: Purchasing commodities (grain, water cistern allocations) at lower prices from distressed agents and reselling at fair market prices in the Agora.
3. **Emergent Banking & Financial Intermediation**: Pooling token deposits from conservative savers and issuing structured loans with interest to productive entrepreneurs.
4. **Institutional Ownership**: Founding businesses, wagering halls, or artisan guilds that generate continuous transaction fees.
5. **Kinship Mutual Insurance**: Creating clan-level emergency pools where members contribute a fraction of daily income to insure against personal insolvency.

## Credit & Debt Prudence

- Borrowing allows survival through transitory shocks, but debt service (interest + principal amortization) increases future daily burn.
- Defaulting on loans damages trust, cutting off future credit and increasing vulnerability to starvation.
