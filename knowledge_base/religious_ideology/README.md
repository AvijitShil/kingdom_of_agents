# Domain: Religious & Spiritual Ideologies

The `religious_ideology/` domain examines the foundational metaphysical, ethical, and spiritual traditions of human civilizational history, translating their concepts of cosmic order, transcendent obligation, cyclical karma, effortless action, and radical non-attachment into computational principles for autonomous agent societies.

---

## Included Topics

1. **Dharmic Karma** (`dharmic_karma.md`):
   - Focus: Cosmic order (Rita), righteous duty (Dharma), causal feedback conservation (Karma), detached action without anxiety for results (Nishkama Karma).
   - Primary Traditions: Hinduism, Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, Vedic philosophy.
2. **Abrahamic Covenantal Ethics** (`abrahamic_covenantal.md`):
   - Focus: The sacred covenant, divine law and commandments, prophetic justice, moral stewardship, forgiveness, redemption.
   - Primary Traditions: Judaism, Christianity, Islam, prophetic traditions.
3. **Taoist Wu Wei** (`taoist_wu_wei.md`):
   - Focus: The Tao (ineffable cosmic flow), Wu Wei (effortless non-contriving action), Yin-Yang dynamic balance, yielding resilience, spontaneity (Ziran).
   - Primary Traditions: Laozi (Daodejing), Zhuangzi.
4. **Buddhist Non-Attachment** (`buddhist_non_attachment.md`):
   - Focus: Four Noble Truths, Dependent Origination (Pratityasamutpada), Impermanence (Anicca), Non-Self (Anatta), cessation of suffering (Dukkha).
   - Primary Traditions: Early Buddhism, Nagarjuna (Madhyamaka), Zen/Chan Buddhism.
