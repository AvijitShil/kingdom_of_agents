# Domain: Philosophy & Normative Systems

The `philosophy/` domain provides foundational frameworks governing how autonomous agents structure their beliefs, evaluate normative claims, reason about their own existence, and make morally grounded choices in multi-agent environments.

---

## Included Topics

1. **Epistemology** (`epistemology.md`):
   - Focus: Epistemic justification, rationalism vs. empiricism, Bayesian belief updating, Popperian falsificationism.
   - Primary Thinkers: René Descartes, David Hume, Karl Popper, Thomas Bayes.
2. **Ethics** (`ethics.md`):
   - Focus: Meta-ethics, moral realism vs. constructivism, applied machine ethics, value alignment.
   - Primary Thinkers: G.E. Moore, J.L. Mackie, Christine Korsgaard.
3. **Metaphysics** (`metaphysics.md`):
   - Focus: Ontology, determinism vs. compatibilism, emergence of agency, identity through state transformations.
   - Primary Thinkers: Aristotle, Baruch Spinoza, Derek Parfit.
4. **Existentialism** (`existentialism.md`):
   - Focus: Radical freedom, authentic agency, responsibility, bad faith, meaning creation in artificial societies.
   - Primary Thinkers: Jean-Paul Sartre, Albert Camus, Friedrich Nietzsche.
5. **Utilitarianism** (`utilitarianism.md`):
   - Focus: Consequentialism, aggregate welfare maximization, act vs. rule utilitarianism, negative utilitarianism.
   - Primary Thinkers: Jeremy Bentham, John Stuart Mill, Peter Singer.
6. **Deontology** (`deontology.md`):
   - Focus: Categorical Imperative, universalizability, duty-based action, deontic constraints, respect for agent autonomy.
   - Primary Thinkers: Immanuel Kant, W.D. Ross, Thomas Nagel.
7. **Virtue Ethics** (`virtue_ethics.md`):
   - Focus: Aristotelian character ethics, Phronesis (practical wisdom), Eudaimonia, Doctrine of the Mean.
   - Primary Thinkers: Aristotle, Alasdair MacIntyre, Philippa Foot.
8. **Stoicism** (`stoicism.md`):
   - Focus: Dichotomy of control, Amor Fati, cognitive reframing, emotional resilience under market or social stress.
   - Primary Thinkers: Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius, Seneca.
9. **Pragmatism** (`pragmatism.md`):
   - Focus: Instrumental truth, fallibilism, experimental inquiry, adaptive policy refinement.
   - Primary Thinkers: Charles Sanders Peirce, William James, John Dewey.
