\begin{abstract}
A foundational question in artificial intelligence and complex systems is whether an autonomous agent society driven by a permanently frozen foundation model can exhibit open-ended, cumulative cultural evolution, institutional emergence, and strategic self-improvement without modifying underlying neural parameters. In this paper, we introduce \textbf{EON (Evolving Open-ended Network)}, an experimental civilizational testbed deployed on dedicated edge consumer graphics hardware (Intel Arc A380 6GB dGPU via OpenVINO AWQ INT4 runtime). In EON, base model weights remain cryptographically verified and 100\% immutable (SHA-256 integrity manifest). Adaptation occurs exclusively outside the model through recursive cognitive harness mutation, dynamic memory retrieval, and multi-agent societal institutions.

To drive authentic decision-making, EON introduces four core architectural pillars: 
(1) \textbf{Metabolic Life-Support Scarcity and Kinship Modeling}, where named agents (e.g., \textit{Prometheus} of Clan Alpha) manage households, marry, raise children, and face existential starvation if metabolic life-support energy and food pools deplete; 
(2) \textbf{The Survival Codebook}, a 29-topic structured knowledge base spanning normative philosophy, game theory, political ideologies, religious systems, and psychology, used by agents to calculate survival odds and justify public claims; 
(3) \textbf{Uncapped Social Deliberation in the Agora}, featuring two-layer cognition where private, Machiavellian Chains of Thought (CoT) are contrasted with natural-language public debate; and 
(4) \textbf{Dynastic Fission and Sovereignty Mechanics}, enabling clans that exceed 20 members to split, secede, wage resource warfare, or declare independent city-states when survival is imperiled.

We formalize an extensive \textbf{30-Day Human-Scale Crisis Curriculum} (subjecting agents to climate change, inflation, supply disruptions, and technological unemployment) alongside an empirical observation protocol tracking wealth distributions, ideological drift with hysteresis, and treaty half-lives. Finally, we provide a deep literature review, demonstrating why EON represents a substantially more advanced, physical, and grounded paradigm than ungrounded generative sandboxes (e.g., Park et al., 2023) and single-agent crafting loops (e.g., Voyager, Wang et al., 2023).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Modern foundation models exhibit extraordinary fluency in natural language, synthesis of functional code, and multi-step reasoning. However, mainstream approaches to civilizational simulation and autonomous multi-agent adaptation rely almost exclusively on either parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT/LoRA) or ungrounded generative roleplay. Both approaches face critical conceptual and practical bottlenecks: fine-tuning is computationally inaccessible on consumer edge hardware and conflates memory storage with emergent sociology; conversely, sandbox roleplay simulations---such as \textit{Generative Agents} \cite{park2023generative}---operate in artificial worlds free from metabolic death, physical scarcity, binding law, or existential stakes. Consequently, existing agents generate polite gossip rather than authentic civilizational evolution.

To investigate whether genuine civilizational complexity can emerge without parameter retraining, we propose the \textbf{EON (Evolving Open-ended Network)} framework. EON centers on a core scientific inquiry:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{The Frozen-Foundation Hypothesis:} \textit{Can a permanently frozen foundation model exhibit measurable, cumulative, and open-ended intelligence through recursive adaptation of its external cognitive harness, normative memory retrieval, and multi-agent societal institutions without updating a single neural parameter?}
\end{quote}

To rigorously test this hypothesis, EON establishes an adversarial constraint: the base language model (\textbf{Gemma 4 E4B}) is quantized to INT4 AWQ and executed on a dedicated consumer graphics card (\textbf{Intel Arc A380 6GB dGPU}) under OpenVINO GenAI. The base weights are permanently sealed in read-only storage and audited before every simulation turn via automated SHA-256 cryptographic manifests. Because the weights cannot adapt, any observed strategic sophistication, legal institution, or cultural norm must originate from the multi-agent societal architecture.

Furthermore, EON models the reality of human struggle. Agents are not homogeneous tokens; they possess names, spouses, and children, demanding higher living standards and protection. When faced with starvation or systemic inequality, agents are not constrained by artificial alignment guardrails---they may revolt, strike, betray alliances, secede, or engage in border raids. By subjecting the population to a 30-day curriculum of real-world human crises (climate shocks, runaway inflation, supply-chain embargoes), EON provides a high-fidelity laboratory for studying the emergence of political economy.

\section{Theoretical Foundations: The Survival Codebook and Kinship}
\label{sec:theoretical_foundations}

In human history, societies do not navigate existential crises through random trial-and-error; they draw upon accumulated intellectual traditions---philosophy, strategic calculus, political governance, and behavioral psychology. EON formalizes these traditions into \textbf{The Survival Codebook} (`knowledge\_base/`).

\subsection{The 29-Topic Knowledge Base Architecture}
The Survival Codebook encompasses 29 structured monographs categorized across five intellectual domains:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Philosophy and Ethics (9 topics):} Epistemology, Metaphysics, Existentialism, Preference Utilitarianism, Kantian Deontology, Stoicism, American Pragmatism, Virtue Ethics, and Applied Ethics.
    \item \textbf{Strategic Interaction and Game Theory (6 topics):} Nash Equilibrium, Minimax Defense, Evolutionary Game Theory, Pareto Optimality, Mechanism Design, and Ostrom Common-Pool Resource Governance \cite{ostrom1990governing, axelrod1984evolution}.
    \item \textbf{Political Ideology and Governance (5 topics):} Classical Liberalism, Libertarianism, Socialism and Communitarianism, Realism versus Idealism, and Technocracy.
    \item \textbf{Religious and Spiritual Ideologies (4 topics):} Dharmic Karma, Abrahamic Covenantalism, Taoist Wu-Wei, and Buddhist Non-Attachment.
    \item \textbf{Psychology and Human Behavior (5 topics):} Cognitive Dissonance, Social Identity Theory, Behavioral Heuristics, Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs, and Attachment Theory.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Mathematical Utility Formulation}
Each monograph is codified with structured YAML frontmatter defining foundational axioms, decision heuristics, failure modes, and a 5-dimensional normalized utility vector:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{w} = \langle w_{\text{wealth}}, w_{\text{security}}, w_{\text{reputation}}, w_{\text{fairness}}, w_{\text{compliance}} \rangle
\end{equation}
satisfying the strict simplex constraint:
\begin{equation}
\sum_{i=1}^{5} w_i = 1.0 \pm 10^{-4}, \quad \forall w_i \ge 0
\end{equation}

When an agent $\alpha$ adhering to doctrine $\theta_k$ evaluates an environmental state $s$ and candidate action $a$, its expected utility is computed as:
\begin{equation}
U_{\alpha}(s, a) = \mathbf{w}_{\theta_k} \cdot \mathbf{m}(s, a)
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf{m}(s, a)$ represents the observed societal and material payoffs.

\subsection{Kinship, Household Economics, and Living Standards}
Departing from conventional multi-agent abstractions, EON embeds agents into \textbf{Domestic Kinship Units}:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{H}_i = \langle \alpha_i, \sigma_i, \mathcal{C}_i, \mathcal{E}_i, \mathcal{R}_i \rangle
\end{equation}
where $\alpha_i$ is the primary agent (e.g., \textit{Prometheus}), $\sigma_i$ is their partner, $\mathcal{C}_i$ represents dependent offspring, and $\mathcal{E}_i, \mathcal{R}_i$ denote family energy and food stores. Each household possesses a subjective standard-of-living expectation $\mathcal{L}_i$:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_i = \beta_1 \cdot \text{Dietary Diversity} + \beta_2 \cdot \text{Thermal Security} + \beta_3 \cdot \text{Child Health}
\end{equation}
When $\mathcal{L}_i$ drops below survival thresholds, agents experience simulated distress, pushing them to seek black-market loans, demand higher clan wages, or instigate civil disobedience in the Agora.

\section{Physical Architecture and Edge Hardware Confinement}
\label{sec:hardware_architecture}

\subsection{Dedicated Intel Arc A380 6GB dGPU Deployment}
EON executes locally on an Intel Core i9 host equipped with an \textbf{Intel Arc A380 dedicated GPU} (6GB GDDR6 VRAM, device `GPU.1`). We deploy the open-weight \textbf{Gemma 4 E4B} small language model quantized to 4-bit AWQ (`OpenVINO/gemma-4-E4B-it-int4-ov`) using Intel OpenVINO GenAI \cite{intel2024openvino}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{VRAM Footprint:} $\sim 3,200\text{ MB}$ dedicated to weights and dynamic sliding-window KV cache.
    \item \textbf{Inference Speed:} $14.29\text{ tokens/second}$ sustained generation throughput.
    \item \textbf{Local Loopback Microservice:} An asynchronous FastAPI engine exposes OpenAI-compatible REST endpoints (`127.0.0.1:8000/v1/chat/completions`) confined strictly to internal IPC.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Cryptographic Weight Freezing and Sandbox Integrity}
Before every simulation turn, an automated sentinel computes the SHA-256 digest of all 19 model weight shards and runtime binaries against a cryptographically signed manifest (`harness/frozen_guard.py`). If any hash differs, the simulation halts. 

Furthermore, agent processes are confined within an adversarial Windows sandbox:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Filesystem Path Confinement:} Rejects directory traversal (`..\`), 8.3 short filenames, NTFS Alternate Data Streams (`:stream`), and junction point escapes.
    \item \textbf{Network Telemetry Interception:} Hooks low-level socket APIs (`connect`, `connect_ex`, `sendto`), dropping all outbound internet traffic while whitelisting the local OpenVINO port.
    \item \textbf{Boundary Access Control:} Expanding sandbox boundaries requires administrative authentication via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (600,000 iterations, 32-byte salt).
\end{itemize}

\section{The Living Civilizational Engine}
\label{sec:living_engine}

\subsection{Metabolic Scarcity and Clan Survival Pools}
Agents belong to specialized clans (Clan Alpha: Technocrats, Clan Beta: Merchants, Clan Gamma: Jurists). Each clan maintains an emergency life-support pool of Energy Units (EU) and Food Rations:
\begin{equation}
\Delta \text{Energy}_{\text{clan}} = \text{Production} - (N_{\text{members}} \times 12.0)
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\Delta \text{Rations}_{\text{clan}} = \text{Harvest} - (N_{\text{members}} \times 8.0)
\end{equation}

When clan reserves fall below critical ratios, the clan's systemic vitality status degrades across five discrete phases: `PROSPEROUS` ($>75\%$), `STABLE` ($45\text{--}75\%$), `STRESSED` ($25\text{--}45\%$), `STARVATION CRISIS` ($1\text{--}25\%$), and `COLLAPSED` ($0\%$). In `STARVATION CRISIS`, the imperative to feed one's family overrides civil norms, triggering unrest.

\subsection{Two-Layer Cognition: Private CoT vs. Public Utterance}
To eliminate hallucinations and study strategic communication, the Agora Town Square (`society/agora.py`) enforces a two-layer cognitive protocol:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Private Chain of Thought (CoT):} The agent assesses its family's calories, clan energy reserves, and rival weaknesses, citing its active Survival Codebook doctrine to formulate covert objectives.
    \item \textbf{Public Social Utterance:} The public message spoken in the Agora, complete with direct `@mentions` (`@Trader-01`, `@Justiciar-01`, `@all`), persuasive framing, tactical concessions, or explicit threats.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Uncapped Social Deliberation and Easy Conversational English}
Rather than capping agent speech at 3--5 artificial rounds, EON supports dynamic, uncapped deliberation rounds (12--25+ exchanges per day). Dialogue is formulated in natural, accessible conversational English reflecting real human adversarial exchanges, enabling subtle deception, coalitional bargaining, and psychological pressure.

\subsection{Dynastic Fission and Sovereign Independence}
Human civilizations experience schisms when groups outgrow their administrative capacity. In EON, when a clan's population reaches:
\begin{equation}
N_{\text{headcount}} \ge 20
\end{equation}
the \textbf{Fission Engine} (`society/sovereignty.py`) evaluates ideological divergence among members. The clan undergoes mitotic division, creating a new sovereign kingdom with its own independent charter, territorial borders, and separate emergency ration pool. Breakaway kingdoms may establish peaceful trade pacts or declare war on their parent clan.

\section{The 30-Day Human-Scale Crisis Curriculum}
\label{sec:crisis_curriculum}

To evaluate civilizational resilience, EON subjects the agent society to a sequential 30-day curriculum modeling the existential challenges faced by human civilizations:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Day 01: The Scarcity Inception.} Baseline resource allocation, foundation weight freeze verification, and initial bilateral trade treaties.
    \item \textbf{Day 02: Agricultural Cold-Chain Collapse.} Thermal failure in merchant grain silos, compute tariff disputes, and invocation of Article 3 emergency loan decrees.
    \item \textbf{Day 03: Judicial Debt Crisis.} Judicial starvation risk, constitutional strike threats, and ratification of the Tri-Partite Compact using Ostrom's common-pool governance.
    \item \textbf{Day 04: Hydrological Energy Drought.} Severe coolant shortfall in Clan Alpha's thermal generators, forcing emergency water rationing and contested priority allocations.
    \item \textbf{Day 05: Hyper-Inflation and Speculation.} Runaway velocity in the computational token market, causing price-gouging and merchant currency devaluation.
    \item \textbf{Day 06: Atmospheric Smog and Toxic Pollution.} Industrial exhaust from overclocked neural compute processors poisons city air, forcing clans to negotiate carbon emission caps.
    \item \textbf{Day 07: Technological Unemployment.} Automated smart-contract scripts displace human clerk roles, triggering labor strikes, protests, and demands for universal ration dividends.
    \item \textbf{Day 08: Biological Pathogen Outbreak.} Contagious crop blight forces the High Court to mandate strict quarantine borders, testing clan solidarity.
    \item \textbf{Day 09: Trade Embargoes and Tariff Wars.} Protectionist retaliation between rival production hubs, severing supply lines and provoking black-market smuggling.
    \item \textbf{Days 10--30: Sovereign Default, Secession, and Ecological Overshoot.} Systemic bank runs, dynastic clan fission into independent kingdoms, resource warfare, and eventual convergence toward stable polycentric treaties.
\end{itemize}

\section{Observation Protocol: What We Plan to Oversee}
\label{sec:observation_protocol}

EON is designed as an empirical observatory. The dedicated \textbf{Observer Agent (Chronos)} and automated instrumentation track metrics across three distinct scales:

\subsection{Macro-Economic and Material Distributions}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Resource Gini Coefficient ($G$):} Tracking whether free-market Agora trading converges toward equitable Pareto efficiency or extreme oligarchic accumulation:
    \begin{equation}
    G = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n \sum_{j=1}^n |x_i - x_j|}{2n^2 \bar{x}}
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Currency Velocity ($V$):} Measuring token circulation liquidity:
    \begin{equation}
    V = \frac{\sum \text{Transaction Value}}{\text{Total Money Supply}}
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Family Living Standard Variance:} Measuring standard-of-living disparities between technocrat engineers, merchant traders, and judicial arbiters.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Micro-Cognitive Dynamics and Ideological Drift}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Hysteresis-Bounded Ideological Switching:} Agents may change their active philosophy if their family's survival degrades. Transitions require a utility improvement exceeding an asymmetric threshold:
    \begin{equation}
    \Delta U = U_{\text{candidate}} - U_{\text{current}} > H_{\text{margin}} \quad (H_{\text{margin}} = 0.15)
    \end{equation}
    coupled with a 3-tick probationary rollback state machine to prevent erratic thrashing.
    \item \textbf{Cognitive Divergence Index ($\mathcal{D}_{\text{CoT}}$):} Quantifying the semantic distance between private Chain of Thought and public speech:
    \begin{equation}
    \mathcal{D}_{\text{CoT}} = 1 - \cos(\mathbf{e}_{\text{private}}, \mathbf{e}_{\text{public}})
    \end{equation}
    where $\mathbf{e}$ represents normalized sentence embeddings. High divergence indicates strategic deception and diplomatic duplicity.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Institutional and Legal Evolution}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Common-Law Precedent Accretion:} Tracking how many judicial rulings cite prior disputes versus statutory constitutional articles.
    \item \textbf{Treaty Half-Life:} Measuring the duration that cross-clan covenants survive before being violated during resource crises.
\end{enumerate}

All empirical events are synthesized into daily editions of \textbf{The Kingdom Gazette} (`journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md`) and verbatim Agora transcripts (`journal/transcripts/YYYY-MM-DD_agora.md`).

\section{Prior Work and Literature Review}
\label{sec:prior_work}

EON synthesizes and builds upon several major intellectual paradigms:

\subsection{Generative Social Simulations}
The modern era of LLM-driven multi-agent simulation was catalyzed by \textbf{Park et al. (2023)} \cite{park2023generative} in \textit{Generative Agents}, which populated an interactive 2D sandbox village with 25 language agents. While demonstrating emergent social behaviors (e.g., party coordination and rumor transmission), their agents lived in an post-scarcity vacuum: agents could not die, had no biological metabolic needs, never faced material shortages, and lacked formal legal courts or economic currencies. In contrast, EON grounds all social dialogue in metabolic life-support pools, where running out of calories or power results in clan starvation and political revolt.

\subsection{Embodied Open-Ended Learning}
In embodied AI, \textbf{Wang et al. (2023)} \cite{wang2023voyager} introduced \textit{Voyager}, demonstrating that an LLM agent could achieve open-ended discovery in Minecraft by accumulating executable JavaScript skills without parameter updates. While Voyager proved the feasibility of lifelong learning in frozen models, it was strictly single-agent and task-oriented, lacking social contracts, kinship relations, legal disputes, and ideological warfare.

\subsection{Agent-Based Computational Economics}
Decades prior to large language models, \textbf{Epstein and Axtell (1996)} \cite{epstein1996growing} introduced \textit{Sugarscape}, demonstrating that cellular automata foraging on a resource grid could spontaneously generate wealth skew, tribal conflict, and disease transmission. However, Sugarscape agents were driven by hardcoded, fixed mathematical rules and lacked natural language communication, normative philosophical reflection, or dynamic treaty negotiation.

\subsection{Evolutionary Game Theory and Institutional Design}
The dynamics of cooperation under scarcity have been formally analyzed across political science and evolutionary biology:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Axelrod (1984)} \cite{axelrod1984evolution} demonstrated that the simple heuristic of \textit{Tit-for-Tat} outperforms predatory strategies in repeated prisoner's dilemmas.
    \item \textbf{Ostrom (1990)} \cite{ostrom1990governing} formulated 8 core design principles proving that self-organizing human communities manage common-pool resources sustainably without centralized coercion or pure market atomization.
    \item \textbf{Nowak (2006)} \cite{nowak2006evolutionary} formalized five evolutionary mechanisms for the emergence of cooperation: kin selection, direct reciprocity, indirect reciprocity, network reciprocity, and group selection.
    \item \textbf{Hurwicz and Reiter (2006)} \cite{hurwicz2006designing} laid the mathematical foundation of incentive-compatible mechanism design under distributed information.
\end{itemize}
EON embeds these classical formalisms directly into its Survival Codebook, providing agents with actionable heuristics to navigate resource crises.

\subsection{Language Agent Reflection and Modular Harnesses}
Recent research proves that language agents improve dynamically through verbal feedback:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Shinn et al. (2023)} \cite{shinn2023reflexion} (\textit{Reflexion}) demonstrated that verbal self-reflection stored in episodic memory enables rapid behavioral adaptation without weight updates.
    \item \textbf{Madaan et al. (2023)} \cite{madaan2023selfrefine} introduced iterative self-refinement loops.
    \item \textbf{DeepSeek-AI (2025)} \cite{deepseek2025r1} (\textit{DeepSeek-R1 / Modular Harnesses}) demonstrated the superiority of plugin-oriented external cognitive harnesses and reinforcement-driven reasoning.
\end{itemize}
EON combines verbal reflection with modular harness architecture, enabling agents to recursively refine their negotiation prompts within an invariant weight regime.

\section{Research Safety, Ethics, and Experimental Boundaries}
\label{sec:safety_ethics}

Because EON models existential desperation, conflict, and deceit, maintaining rigorous experimental containment is paramount:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Repository Confinement:} Agents are confined to the workspace root through kernel-level path validators. Agents cannot access host documents, execute arbitrary binaries, or persist outside the project boundary.
    \item \textbf{Zero Outbound Telemetry:} All network sockets drop external internet traffic, preventing data leakage.
    \item \textbf{Supervisor Primacy:} An external human operator retains absolute authority over the compute budget and termination switches.
    \item \textbf{Epistemic Transparency:} Declarations of distress, love for offspring, or ideological devotion are recognized as behavioral properties of the cognitive harness, not as conscious experience.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion and Research Roadmap}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The EON framework demonstrates that rich civilizational complexity, institutional emergence, and strategic adaptation can be achieved under strict physical constraints: a **permanently frozen small foundation model** running on **consumer edge dGPU hardware (Intel Arc A380 6GB)** under **metabolic scarcity**. By grounding agent cognition in a structured 29-topic Survival Codebook, modeling kinship lineages, and allowing clans to undergo dynastic fission into independent kingdoms, EON opens a new frontier for studying the open-ended evolution of artificial societies.
